# Hazard Reference Data Sources & Provenance

## Data Sources & Authoritative References
- **GHS Reference Framework**: UN Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals (GHS), 11th Revised Edition (2025). Source identifier: `UN-GHS-REV11-2025`.
- **Publisher**: United Nations Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE) Committee of Experts on the Transport of Dangerous Goods and GHS.
- **Framework Scope**: `INTERNATIONAL_REFERENCE` (Universal GHS Reference Benchmark).
- **Implementation Jurisdictions**: `EU` (`ECHA-CL-INVENTORY-2025`), `UNITED_STATES` (`OSHA-HCS-2025` regulatory implementation standard), `SUPPLIER_SPECIFIC`, `UNSPECIFIED`.

## Licensing & Reuse Policy
- Sourced GHS hazard class codes, category designations, pictogram identifiers, standard H-statements, and P-statements represent official international reference standard metadata released by the United Nations.
- No proprietary SDS documents or commercial vendor safety data sheets are stored or reproduced.
- External internet access is zero at runtime; all reference dictionary data is checked in under `chemistry-engine/src/main/resources/chemistry-data/`.
